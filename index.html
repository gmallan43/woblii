How it works

Built around capacity,
not time.

Most planners assume you are the same person every day. You are not. Woblii knows this.

1
Check in honestly
Every morning, tell Woblii who you actually are today. How you slept. Whether you have eaten. Your medication. How your body feels. This is not box ticking. It changes what the day asks of you.
2
Your companion shows up
One of five companions appears, matched to your state. Grumble on flat days. Pebble when you are fragile. Colin when you are steady. Crunchy when you are energised. Pip when you are lit up. Each one responds to what is actually happening.
3
The energy jar, the enough line
Tasks are sized by energy cost, not time. Your jar fills up. The enough line shows you exactly when the day is already a success. Everything above it is bonus.
4
Close with kindness
Every day ends with two questions. One thing that happened, however small. What tomorrow's version of you needs to know. No scoring. No review. No evidence of failure.
